# Phase 2B2 prior-experience correspondence audit

This audit was completed before new Phase 2B2 design or execution.  It is a
correspondence audit, not a transfer of authority, truth, or proof.

## L7 correspondence

Exact identity, hashes, bindings, and deterministic replay keys prevent silent
substitution; none grants authority.  Phase 2B2 therefore binds the full Genie
token, request/schema hashes, Codex path/version/hash, model, effort, service
tier, sandbox, and worker role into one durable logical-operation identity.
Reuse is terminal UNKNOWN and cannot produce a replacement invocation.

## L2 correspondence

Controller progression, worker execution, process observation, review, and
destination verification remain separate.  The child receives no Genie token,
queue/owned-root/registry path, mutation surface, callback, or authority secret.
Child/model output remains untrusted data.  Acceptance would require a fresh
owned-destination readback after deterministic parent review.

## Genie correspondence

After reservation or any process-entry attempt, uncertainty preserves the
original state and forbids retry, refund, reset, replacement identity, or
alternate launcher.  Only deterministic failure before process-entry attempt
may be labeled REJECT.  No session driver, reviewer model, event-application
loop, or autonomous progression is exposed.

## EXP010 correspondence

Only evidence-discipline lessons transfer: exact executable/commit/run/attempt
identity, raw evidence retention, chronology, and explicit failure reporting.
No EXP010 execution, semantics, authority, promotion, or claim transfers.

## Independence boundary

Same-host and same-provider/model-family observations are common-cause evidence,
not independent provenance.  The pilot makes no production, sandbox, hostile
pre-assignment, filesystem, network-isolation, or independent-proof claim.
