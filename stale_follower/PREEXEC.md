Initial internal critique before primary-source research: stopping a follower would stop its client service, so it cannot supply a stale response. Instead, peer-only isolation must leave its process and client listener alive. Closing only incoming connections is insufficient because peer streams are bidirectional. Existing streams and new incoming/outgoing peer connections involving that member must be blocked. Any sender labeling is merely a test-routing observation, never cryptographic authorization.
